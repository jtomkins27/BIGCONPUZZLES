FIELD MANUAL

How to pull the Big Con

1. Move. Click a node connected to your current position. You get two moves each turn, following the lines.

2. Claim. End movement on an unowned node and pay its price to take control. Owned nodes pay income at the start of your turn.

3. Use your action. Claim a node, lay low for cash, sell intel, or audit a rival. Rival nodes cost more to seize, so choose your timing.

4. Watch the market. Events change income and node prices. High heat risks a regulator raid, which costs money and skips your next income.

5. Win. Reach $35 and control at least one node in all six categories. Solo mode uses an AI opponent. Local multiplayer is pass-and-play on one device.
